# TURN OR RESTRAIN CREATURES

Some god's voices can use their connection to energy and elemental planes and
channel those forces to halt, drive off or destroy creatures antithetical
to them. Similarly, some have the ability to halt, awe, control or bolster
creatures aligned to them.

Regardless of the effect, the general term for the activity is
"turning." When attempting to exercise their divine control over these
creatures, characters make turning checks.

## Turning Checks

Turning is a supernatural ability that a character can perform as a standard
action. It does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

Turning is considered an attack for the purposes of abilities that interact with
attacks.

**Times per Day:** Each turning attempt requires spending a godly spark (see
God's Voice class feature). Usually, domains that grant turning ability increase
a god's voice's sparks.

**Range:** You turn the closest turnable creature first, and you can't
turn creatures that are more than 60 feet away or that have total cover
relative to you. You don't need line of sight to a target, but you do
need line of effect.

**Turning Check:** The first thing you do is roll a turning check to see
how powerful a creature you can turn. This is a Charisma check
(1d20 + your Charisma modifier). Table: Turning Check gives you the Hit
Dice of the most powerful creature you can affect, relative to your level.
On a given turning attempt, you can turn no creature whose Hit
Dice exceed the result on this table.

### Table: Turning Check

| Turning Check Result | Most Powerful Creature Affected<br>(Maximum Hit Dice) |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 or lower           | Voice's level -3                                      |
| 3–5                  | Voice's level -2                                      |
| 6–8                  | Voice's level -1                                      |
| 9–11                 | Voice's level                                         |
| 12–14                | Voice's level +1                                      |
| 15–17                | Voice's level +2                                      |
| 18–20                | Voice's level +3                                      |
| 21 or higher         | Voice's level +4                                      |

**Turning Damage:** If your roll on Table: Turning Check is high enough
to let you turn at least some of the creatures within 60 feet, roll 4d6, keeping
the highest three, + your Charisma modifier to determine your turning damage
that you'll spend by comparing creatures' Hit Dice against your effective
turning level (expressed as *TL* in the table).

### Table: Turning Damage

| Creature's Hit Dice | Turning damage to affect |
| ------------------- | ------------------------ |
| TL-7 and below¹     | ½                        |
| TL-6¹               | 1                        |
| TL-5¹               | 2                        |
| TL-4¹               | 3                        |
| TL-3                | 4                        |
| TL-2                | 6                        |
| TL-1                | 8                        |
| TL                  | 10                       |
| TL+1                | 12                       |
| TL+2                | 14                       |
| TL+3                | 16                       |
| TL+4                | 18                       |

1. You can instead destroy these creatures, see below.

You may skip over already turned creatures that are still within range, so
that you do not waste your turning capacity on them.

**Effect and Duration of Turning:** Turned creatures flee from you by the
best and fastest means available to them. They flee for 10 rounds (1
minute). If they cannot flee, they cower (giving any attack rolls
against them a +2 bonus). If you approach within 10 feet of them,
however, they overcome being turned and act normally. (You can stand
within 10 feet without breaking the turning effect—you just can't
approach them.) You can attack them with ranged attacks (from at least
10 feet away), and others can attack them in any fashion, without
breaking the turning effect.

**Destroying Creatures:** If you have 4 or more levels than the creature's Hit
Dice, you can instead destroy them by spending 2 additional damage per target.

Destroyed outsiders and elementals are banished to their home planes, undead are
disintegrated into fine dust, and earthly creatures such as dragons simply die.

### Turning Example

Koran, a 7th level voice of the sun god faces five ghasts (4 HD each). To turn them,
she first needs to make a turning check but skips it because even the lowest result
is enough turn them.

She then rolls 4d6+Charisma modifier of +1, getting 6, 4, 3 and 6.
Discarding the lowest die of 3, her final turning damage becomes 17.
Since ghasts are TL-3, she needs to spend 4 damage to affect a single one.
Her turning damage is not enough to turn all five, so only four are turned and
one remains unaffected.

## Restraining Creatures

Some god's voices control the energies that are embodied in certain creatures.
They still make turning checks and roll turning damage as normal, but creatures
that would be turned are restrained, and those that would be destroyed are
commanded instead.

**Restrained:** A restrained creature cowers as if in awe (attack rolls against
the creature get a +2 bonus). The effect lasts 10 rounds.

**Commanded:** A commanded creature is under the mental control of the
god's voice. The god's voice must take a standard action to give mental orders
to a commanded creature. At any one time, the god's voice may command any number
of creatures whose total Hit Dice do not exceed their level. They may voluntarily
relinquish command on any commanded creatures in order to command new ones.

**Dispelling Turning:** A god's voice with the ability to restrain creatures may
instead channel their energy to dispel an opposed god's voice's turning effect.
The restraining voice makes a turning check with the difference between turning
levels as a bonus or penalty (it is easier to dispel the turning of a lower
level god's voice). Then roll turning damage as normal to determine how many Hit
Dice worth of creatures are released from the effects of turning.

**Bolstering Undead:** A god's voice may also bolster creatures against turning
in advance. They make a turning check as if attempting to restrain the creature,
but the Hit Dice result on Table: Turning Check becomes the creatures' effective
Hit Dice as far as turning is concerned (provided the result is higher than the
creatures' actual Hit Dice). The bolstering lasts 10 rounds.

A god's voice can bolster themselves in this manner if they are a creature type
subject to turning.

## Holy Champions and Undead

Beginning at 4th level, holy champions can turn and destroy undead creatures as
if they were god's voices with Life domain. Their effective turning level equals
their holy champion level-2 (or 2 less than sum of their holy champion and god's
voice levels, if they also got Life domain) but they get to roll an additional
1d6 to determine their turning damage, in effect taking best 4 out of 5d6.
