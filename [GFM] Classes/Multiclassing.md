# Multiclass Characters

A character may take levels in a second class, becoming a multiclass character. This second class must be their race's favored class, or any class they like if they were already their favored class. Multiclassing improves a character’s versatility at the expense of focus.

A character can always take levels in prestige classes as long as they meet the requirements.

## Races and Favored Classes

Each race has a favored class that they can freely multiclass into. These are:

* Human: Any
* Dwarf: ~~Fighter or~~ weapon master
* Elf: ~~Wizard or~~ magician
* Gnome: Sorcerer
* Half-elf: ~~Bard or~~ wanderer
* Half-orc: ~~Barbarian or~~ brute
* Halfling: Rogue

## Class and Level Features

As a general rule, the abilities of a multiclass character are the sum of the abilities of each of the character’s classes. However, there are some exceptions, things to note and keep in mind.

* Skills: while maximum rank is 3 + character level for both classes' class skills, classes determine whether a skill costs one or two skill points to increase a rank. For example, a character with rogue and sorcerer levels can increase both Open Lock and Spellcraft skills to 3+character level, but takes 2 skill points to increase Spellcraft when rogue level increases or to increase Open Lock when sorcerer level increases.
* Armor and shield proficiencies: if your second class has any armor proficiency, you take one lesser proficiency. For example, a mystic warrior taking a ~~paladin or~~ holy champion level would be proficient in light and medium armor, not heavy. Similarly, you don't gain shield proficiency even if your second class has it.
* Feats and ability increases: they depend on your total levels, not on any individual class's level.
* Class features:
  * If a character gains uncanny dodge from their second class, they instead gain improved uncanny dodge and an attacker cares about their total level when determining if they can be flanked.
  * While a magician does not have a familiar, magician levels stack with sorcerer to determine familiar abilities.
