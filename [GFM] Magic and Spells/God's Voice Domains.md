## GOD'S VOICE DOMAINS

### AIR DOMAIN

**Granted Powers:** Increase your godly sparks by 3.

You can turn or destroy earth creatures; restrain, command, or bolster air creatures.
This granted power is a supernatural ability.

#### Air Domain Spells

1. **Obscuring Mist:** Fog surrounds you.
1. **Wind Wall:** Deflects arrows, smaller creatures, and gases.
1. **Gaseous Form:** Subject becomes insubstantial and can fly slowly.\
   **Water Breathing:** Subjects can breathe underwater.
1. **Air Walk:** Subject treads on air as if solid (climb at 45-degree angle).
1. **Control Winds:** Change wind direction and speed.
1. **Chain Lightning:** 1d6/level damage; 1 secondary bolt/level each deals half damage.
1. **Control Weather:** Changes weather in local area.
1. **Whirlwind:** Cyclone deals damage and can pick up creatures.
1. **Elemental Swarm\*:** Summons multiple elementals.

\*Cast as an air spell only.

### ANIMAL DOMAIN

**Granted Powers:** Increase your godly sparks by 2.

You can use *speak with animals* as a spell-like ability by spending a spark.

Add Knowledge (nature) to your list of god's voice class skills.

#### Animal Domain Spells

1. **Calm Animals:** Calms (2d4 + level) HD of animals.
1. **Hold Animal:** Paralyzes one animal for 1 round/level.
1. **Dominate Animal:** Subject animal obeys silent mental commands.
1. **Summon Nature’s Ally IV\*:** Calls creature to fight.
1. **Commune with Nature:** Learn about terrain for 1 mile/level.
1. **Antilife Shell:** 10-ft. field hedges out living creatures.
1. **Animal Shapes:** One ally/level polymorphs into chosen animal.
1. **Summon Nature’s Ally VIII\*:** Calls creature to fight.
1. **Shapechange <sup>F</sup>:** Transforms you into any creature, and change forms once per round.

\*Can only summon animals.

### CHAOS DOMAIN

**Granted Power:** You cast chaos spells at +1 caster level.

#### Chaos Domain Spells

1. **Protection from Law:** +2 to AC and saves, counter mind control, hedge out elementals and outsiders.
1. **Shatter:** Sonic vibration damages objects or crystalline creatures.
1. **Magic Circle against Law:** As *protection* spells, but 10-ft. radius and 10 min./level.
1. **Chaos Hammer:** Damages and staggers lawful creatures.
1. **Dispel Law:** +4 bonus against attacks by lawful creatures.
1. **Animate Objects:** Objects attack your foes.
1. **Word of Chaos:** Kills, *confuses,* stuns, or deafens nonchaotic subjects.
1. **Cloak of Chaos <sup>F</sup>:** +4 to AC, +4 resistance, SR 25 against lawful spells.
1. **Summon Monster IX\*:** Calls extraplanar creature to fight for you.

\*Cast as a chaos spell only.

### DESTRUCTION DOMAIN

**Granted Power:** Increase your godly sparks by 2.

You gain the smite power, the supernatural ability to
make a single melee attack with a +4 bonus on attack rolls and a bonus
on damage rolls equal to your god's voice level (if you hit). You must
declare the smite before making the attack. You can use this ability by
spending two sparks.

#### Destruction Domain Spells

1. **Inflict Light Wounds:** Touch attack, 1d8 damage +1/level (max +5).
1. **Shatter:** Sonic vibration damages objects or crystalline creatures.
1. **Contagion:** Infects subject with chosen disease.
1. **Inflict Critical Wounds:** Touch attack, 4d8 damage +1/level (max +20).
1. **Inflict Light Wounds, Mass:** Deals 1d8 damage +1/level to any creatures.
1. **Disintegrate:** Makes one creature or object vanish.
1. **Destruction <sup>F</sup>:** Kills subject and destroys remains.
1. **Earthquake:** Intense tremor shakes 80-ft.-radius.
1. **Implosion:** Kills one creature/round.

You can turn or destroy air creatures; restrain, command, or bolster earth creatures.

### FIRE DOMAIN

You can turn or destroy cold or water creatures; restrain, command, or bolster fire creatures.

### KNOWLEDGE DOMAIN

**Granted Power:** Add all Knowledge skills to your list of god's voice class skills.
You also gain 1 extra skill point at each level (multiplied by 4 at first level, as normal)
that can only be spent on Knowledge skills. If you access this domain later in your career,
gain these skill points retroactively.

You cast divination spells at +1 caster level.

#### Knowledge Domain Spells

1. **Detect Secret Doors:** Reveals hidden doors within 60 ft.
1. **Detect Thoughts:** Allows “listening” to surface thoughts.
1. **Clairaudience/Clairvoyance:** Hear or see at a distance for 1 min./level.
1. **Divination <sup>M</sup>:** Provides useful advice for specific proposed actions.
1. **True Seeing <sup>M</sup>:** Lets you see all things as they really are.
1. **Find the Path:** Shows most direct way to a location.
1. **Legend Lore <sup>M</sup> <sup>F</sup>:** Lets you learn tales about a person, place, or thing.
1. **Discern Location:** Reveals exact location of creature or object.
1. **Foresight:** “Sixth sense” warns of impending danger.

### LIFE DOMAIN

**Granted Powers:** Increase your godly sparks by 3.

You can turn or destroy undead creatures. This granted power is a supernatural ability.

#### Life Domain Spells

1. **Cure Light Wounds:** Cures 1d8 damage +1/level (max +5).
1. **Cure Moderate Wounds:** Cures 2d8 damage +1/level (max +10).
1. **Cure Serious Wounds:** Cures 3d8 damage +1/level (max +15).

1. **Cure Critical Wounds, Mass:** Cures 4d8 damage +1/level (max +40) for many creatures.
1. **Heal, Mass:** As *heal*, but with several subjects.

### LUCK DOMAIN

**Granted Power:** Increase your godly sparks by 3.

You gain the power of good fortune, an extraordinary ability that allows you to
reroll one roll that you have just made before the DM declares whether the roll
results in success or failure. You must take the result of the reroll, even if
it’s worse than the original roll. You can use this ability by spending two sparks.

#### Luck Domain Spells

1. **Entropic Shield:** Ranged attacks against you have 20% miss chance.
1. **Aid:** +1 on attack rolls, +1 against fear, 1d8 temporary hp +1/level (max +10).
1. **Protection from Energy:** Absorb 12 points/level of damage from one kind of energy.
1. **Freedom of Movement:** Subject moves normally despite impediments.
1. **Break Enchantment:** Frees subjects from enchantments, alterations, curses, and petrification.
1. **Mislead:** Turns you invisible and creates illusory double.
1. **Spell Turning:** Reflect 1d4+6 spell levels back at caster.
1. **Moment of Prescience:** You gain insight bonus on single attack roll, check, or save.
1. **Miracle <sup>X</sup>:** Requests a deity’s intercession.

### MAGIC DOMAIN

**Granted Power:** You can activate scrolls, wands and staves;
as well as any other magic items with spell completion or spell trigger
activation method like a magician can. For the purpose of using scrolls and staves,
your magician level is your god's voice level -4, or 4 less than sum of your
god's voice and magician levels if you have levels in both classes.

#### Magic Domain Spells

1. **Magic Aura:** Alters object’s magic aura.
1. **Identify:** Determines properties of magic item.
1. **Dispel Magic:** Cancels magical spells and effects.
1. **Imbue with Spell Ability:** Transfer spells to subject.
1. **Spell Resistance:** Subject gains SR 12 + level.
1. **Antimagic Field:** Negates magic within 10 ft.
1. **Spell Turning:** Reflect 1d4+6 spell levels back at caster.
1. **Protection from Spells <sup>M</sup> <sup>F</sup>:** Confers +8 resistance bonus.
1. **Mage’s Disjunction:** Dispels magic, disenchants magic items.

### PLANT DOMAIN

**Granted Powers:** Increase your godly sparks by 3.

You can restrain, command, or bolster plant creatures. This granted power is a supernatural ability.

Add Knowledge (nature) to your list of god's voice class skills.

#### Plant Domain Spells

1. **Entangle:** Plants entangle everyone in 40-ft.-radius.
1. **Barkskin:** Grants +2 (or higher) enhancement to natural armor.
1. **Plant Growth:** Grows vegetation, improves crops.
1. **Command Plants:** Sway the actions of one or more plant creatures.
1. **Wall of Thorns:** Thorns damage anyone who tries to pass.
1. **Repel Wood:** Pushes away wooden objects.
1. **Animate Plants:** One or more trees animate and fight for you.
1. **Control Plants:** Control actions of one or more plant creatures.
1. **Shambler:** Summons 1d4+2 shambling mounds to fight for you.

### PROTECTION DOMAIN

**Granted Power:** You can spend your godly spark to grant someone within your
reach +2 bonus to a saving throw they just made, but before the result is announced.

You can also spend 2 sparks to grant someone you touch (a standard action)
a *protective ward* that lasts 1 hour as a supernatural ability.
Someone under this *ward* can lose it to gain +2 to a
saving throw they just made, but before the result is announced.

#### Protection Domain Spells

1. **Sanctuary:** Opponents can’t attack you, and you can’t attack.
1. **Shield Other <sup>F</sup>:** You take half of subject’s damage.
1. **Protection from Energy:** Absorb 12 points/level of damage from one kind of energy.
1. **Spell Immunity:** Subject is immune to one spell per four levels.
1. **Spell Resistance:** Subject gains SR 12 + level.
1. **Antimagic Field:** Negates magic within 10 ft.
1. **Repulsion:** Creatures can’t approach you.
1. **Mind Blank:** Subject is immune to mental/emotional magic and scrying.
1. **Prismatic Sphere:** As *prismatic wall*, but surrounds on all sides.

### STRENGTH DOMAIN

**Granted Power:** Increase your godly sparks by 2.

You can perform feats of strength as a supernatural ability. As a free action,
you gain a bonus equal to your god's voice level to your damage rolls (or 1½ that
if you use a two-handed weapon), to Climb, Jump and Swim checks and Strength checks
that lasts for 1 round. You can use this ability by spending three sparks.

#### Strength Domain Spells

1. **Enlarge Person:** Humanoid creature doubles in size.
1. **Bull’s Strength:** Subject gains +4 to Str for 1 min./level.
1. **Magic Vestment:** Armor or shield gains +1 enhancement per four levels.
1. **Spell Immunity:** Subject is immune to one spell per four levels.
1. **Righteous Might:** Your size increases, and you gain combat bonuses.
1. **Stoneskin <sup>M</sup>:** Ignore 10 points of damage per attack.
1. **Grasping Hand:** Large hand provides cover, pushes, or grapples.
1. **Clenched Fist:** Large hand provides cover, pushes, or attacks your foes.
1. **Crushing Hand:** Large hand provides cover, pushes, or crushes your foes.

### SUN DOMAIN

**Granted Power:** You gain the power to conjure a drop of sun by spending a
spark. This drop floats around your head, sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius
(and dim light for an additional 20 feet) for an hour. Radius increase to 30-foot
at 6th level and to 40-foot at 11th level.

You also gain +1 Hit Points for every god's voice level.

#### Sun Domain Spells

1. **Endure Elements:** Exist comfortably in hot or cold environments.
1. **Daylight:** 60-ft. radius of bright light.
1. **Searing Light:** Ray deals 1d8/two levels, more against undead.
1. **Fire Shield:** Creatures attacking you take fire damage; you’re protected from heat or cold.
1. **Flame Strike:** Smite foes with divine fire (1d6/level damage).
1. **Fire Seeds:** Acorns and berries become grenades and bombs.
1. **Sunbeam:** Beam blinds and deals 4d6 damage.
1. **Sunburst:** Blinds all within 10 ft., deals 6d6 damage.
1. **Prismatic Sphere:** As *prismatic wall*, but surrounds on all sides.

### TRAVEL DOMAIN

**Granted Powers:** Increase your godly sparks by 1.

By spending one spark, you gain the ability to act normally regardless of magical
effects that impede movement as if you were affected by the spell *freedom of movement*
for 5 rounds. This granted power is a supernatural ability.

Add Survival to your list of god's voice class skills.

#### Travel Domain Spells

1. **Longstrider:** Increases your speed.
1. **Locate Object:** Senses direction toward object (specific or type).
1. **Fly:** Subject flies at speed of 60 ft.
1. **Dimension Door:** Teleports you short distance.
1. **Teleport:** Instantly transports you as far as 100 miles/level.
1. **Find the Path:** Shows most direct way to a location.
1. **Teleport, Greater:** As *teleport,* but no range limit and no off-target arrival.
1. **Phase Door:** Creates an invisible passage through wood or stone.
1. **Astral Projection <sup>M</sup>:** Projects you and companions onto Astral Plane.

1. **Nondetection <sup>M</sup>:** Hides subject from divination, scrying.

1. **Screen:** Illusion hides area from vision, scrying.
1. **Polymorph Any Object:** Changes any subject into anything else.
1. **Time Stop:** You act freely for 1d4+1 rounds.
